# Data policy and acknowledgments

Users supply their own Space-Track credentials through `SPACETRACK_USER` and
`SPACETRACK_PASSWORD`. The package contains and redistributes no third-party
orbital data, TLEs, ZTF images, Gaia catalogs, or derived light curves. All
downloads stay in git-ignored local cache folders. Do not publish orbital
elements or credentials in bug reports, notebooks, or repository commits.

The following acknowledgment wording is reproduced from the project's ZTF GEO
technical note. Include it when publishing work that uses the relevant data.
